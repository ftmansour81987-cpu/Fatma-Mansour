## 8. COST JUSTIFICATION & VALUE FOR MONEY

### 8.1 Personnel (€390,000 - 75%)

**Justification:**
- Research quality depends on experienced team
- 3 FTE equivalent allows deep technical work plus stakeholder engagement
- Salaries competitive for EU research context (€35-50k research associate level)
- Includes all statutory contributions, benefits, training

**Value:** High-quality research outputs, international publication record, policy impact

### 8.2 Computational Infrastructure (€85,000 - 16%)

**Justification:**
- GPU servers essential for LSTM, neural networks, fuzzy logic
- Cloud flexibility allows cost optimization
- Year 1 heavy investment; Years 2-3 maintenance focus
- Realistic for processing large financial transaction datasets

**Value:** Reproducible ML models, scalable solutions for multiple cities

### 8.3 Data Acquisition (€60,000 - 11%)

**Justification:**
- Financial data access requires privacy compliance & institutional agreements
- Cyber threat intelligence commercial feeds (€500-1000/month)
- Remote sensing licenses (Sentinel-2, Landsat)
- No alternative for obtaining real-world datasets

**Value:** Authentic data for real-world relevance; comparative analysis across cities

### 8.4 Travel (€50,000 - 10%)

**Justification:**
- Stakeholder engagement critical for policy impact
- Distributed team across multiple European cities
- Conference participation essential for research dissemination
- 3-year span: €50k ≈ €16k/year (modest for international research)

**Value:** Policy integration, publication visibility, practitioner collaboration

### 8.5 Dissemination (€30,000 - 6%)

**Justification:**
- Open access publication fees: €1,500/article standard
- Toolkit development requires documentation & training
- Policy briefs & implementation guides add applied value
- Budget covers 5+ journal articles + books + policy outputs

**Value:** Research impact beyond academia; practitioner accessibility; policy adoption

## 10. BUDGET MANAGEMENT & REPORTING

### 10.1 Financial Governance

**Responsibility:**
- Lead Researcher: Overall budget oversight
- Research Finance Officer: Monthly reporting, expense tracking
- Project Administrator: Travel approvals, procurement

**Quarterly Reviews:**
- Expenditure against planned budget
- Variance analysis
- Contingency reallocation if needed

**Annual Audits:**
- External audit compliance (if FLF requires)
- Financial reporting to Fellowship administration

### 10.2 Cost Control Measures

1. **Competitive Procurement:** Multiple quotes for equipment/services
2. **Cloud Cost Optimization:** Reserved instances, auto-scaling policies
3. **Travel Efficiency:** Combined visits where possible, virtual meetings
4. **Open-Source Tools:** Priority for free/open software (R, Python, QGIS)
5. **Data Sharing:** Leverage existing municipal partnerships to reduce acquisition costs
